The sequence to rehearse

Rice cakes→ Hydration→ Swim→ Carb/electrolyte bottle→ Gel→ Water→ Gel→ Brick / run
Practice the whole sequence, not just what you eat — the goal is for race day to feel routine. Don't introduce anything new (gels, breakfast, supplements, big carb jumps) between now and Oct 11.

How to think about the Olympic

Your sprint was 1:24:30; the Olympic is roughly double the bike/run distance. But with the half marathon only two weeks later, treat the Olympic as a supported long brick workout that happens to have a race number on it.

If you finish thinking "I definitely could have gone harder," that's a successful Olympic. You then get two weeks to recover and let your running fitness show up for the half.
